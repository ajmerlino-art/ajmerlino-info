Leadership

Leadership.

My career spans arts education, academic administration, workforce development, and learning innovation. In each role, the work has been to give creative practice strong programs, trusted relationships, and a life beyond the classroom.

The work

The arts belong at the center of public life.

Creative education is strongest when teaching, research, performance, production, and community engagement reinforce one another. Professional opportunity gives students a way to use what they have learned and signals that a college takes their ambitions seriously. My role is to help people deepen their practice and connect it with the audiences, communities, and work beyond the college.

2015–2022 · Albright College

Build programs and pathways together.

01

Music Industry Studies

I launched and led a major that grew 525% in five years while guiding a department of 8 full-time and 12 adjunct faculty. The program joined creative, technical, and business learning without losing sight of the students behind the enrollment numbers.

02

Student success at scale

As Associate Dean, I worked with 7 direct reports and 12 staff across career development, experiential learning, student employment, and the 13th Street Educational Partnership. Together, we created 4,000+ hands-on opportunities, raised positive outcomes from 84% to 91%, and reached a 100% internship-acquisition rate within four years.

03

Industry and civic relationships

Relationships with Atlantic Records, Warner Music Group, Universal Music Publishing, ASM Global, and Live Nation brought outside expertise into the life of the program. The Music Business Forum gave students a way to meet practitioners and make sense of a changing field.

Bring the field into the program.

03

Experiential learning and professional development

As Associate Vice President and Assistant Professor, I led experiential learning and student professional development, building applied opportunities with partners across the region.
