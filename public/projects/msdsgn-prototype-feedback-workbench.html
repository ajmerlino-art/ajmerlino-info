Interactive portfolio demonstration

Prototype Feedback Workbench

Purpose: make formative UX testing available earlier in a master's-level design course. Students submit early wireframes, receive structured synthetic user-testing data from multiple user perspectives, analyze the patterns, and use the evidence to decide what to revise or test next.

My role: Product design, UX and learning experience design, feedback framework, synthetic-user workflow, and integration into the student design process.

Portfolio note: The interface shown here is a wireframe recreation of a deployed system. It preserves the core workflow and interaction model while removing proprietary data, branding, and production details.
